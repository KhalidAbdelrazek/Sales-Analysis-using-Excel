# Excel Sales Analytics Dashboard

A fully interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** built entirely in **Microsoft Excel** to analyze 60,000+ transactions and extract actionable business insights. This project demonstrates the power of Excel as a **Business Intelligence (BI) tool**, without using Power BI or Tableau.

---

## 📊 Features

- Interactive Dashboard UI with **slicers** and **dynamic KPIs**
- **Power Pivot** & **Data Modeling** for handling large datasets
- **Pivot Tables** for summarizing data by year, product, customer, and geography
- Key Metrics:
  - Revenue
  - Total Profit
  - Profit Margin
  - Units Sold
  - Transactions
- Insight sections:
  - Profit concentration by product
  - Customer behavior by weekdays/weekends
  - Geographic profit distribution
  - Age segment analysis
  - Product concentration (Pareto effect)

## 💡 Key Insights from the Dashboard

- **Explosive Growth**:  
  Revenue grew from $30M in 2005 → $108M in 2008  
  Transactions increased from 923 → 34K (37× growth)

- **Profit Doesn’t Follow Volume**:  
  - Cheap products → 75% of transactions, only 5% of profit  
  - Expensive products → 25% of transactions, 95% of profit  

- **Weekdays Drive Business**:  
  71.8% of profit happens on weekdays  

- **Geographic Concentration**:  
  USA & Australia generate ~60% of total profit  

- **High-Value Customers**:  
  Customers aged 50+ contribute ~40% of total profit  

- **Product Concentration (Pareto Effect)**:  
  Top 5 products generate 31.5% of total profit
